ICRDS 2027: International Conference on Reproducible Data Systems

FIXTURE NOTICE: this is a fictional conference used only for automated tests and demonstrations of the platform. It is not a real call for papers.

ICRDS 2027 will be held in Lisbon, Portugal. Accepted papers will be published in the conference proceedings by the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM).

Topics of Interest

  • Reproducibility of data-intensive experiments
  • Provenance capture and lineage systems
  • Benchmark design and evaluation methodology

Research Papers Track

Research papers must not exceed 10 pages, excluding references and appendices. Submissions must use the ACM primary article template (acmart, sigconf option).

The review process is double-blind. Submissions must be anonymized: remove author names, affiliations and acknowledgments, and refer to your own prior work in the third person.

The abstract must not exceed 250 words.

Supplementary material up to 50 MB may be uploaded together with the paper; reviewers are not obliged to consult it.

Short Papers Track

Short papers are limited to 4 pages, excluding references. Short papers follow the same anonymization rules as research papers.

Important Dates

  • Abstract registration (mandatory): Thursday, January 14, 2027 (AoE)
  • Full paper submission: January 21, 2027 (AoE)
  • Author response (rebuttal) period: March 3-5, 2027
  • Notification of acceptance: March 20, 2027
  • Camera-ready deadline: April 15, 2027 at 23:59 PDT
  • Author registration deadline: May 1, 2027

Submission Policies

Papers submitted to ICRDS 2027 must not be under review elsewhere, and must not have been previously published in a peer-reviewed venue. Dual submission will result in rejection.

Posting a preprint on arXiv is permitted, provided the title differs from the submitted version.

Authors may use generative AI tools for language editing; any other use must be disclosed in the acknowledgments or as otherwise specified by the publisher.

Studies involving human subjects must state whether ethics approval was obtained from an institutional review board.

Papers are submitted through HotCRP at https://icrds27.hotcrp.example/.
